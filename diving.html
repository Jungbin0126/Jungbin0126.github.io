Underwater observations

I'm a NASE Divemaster and Rescue Diver with 100+ logged dives, and I've worked as an assistant dive instructor in South Korea and the Philippines. Diving in the Channel Islands is what got me into kelp research.

Catalina Island

Catalina sits about 20 miles off the Southern California coast. Its water is clearer than the mainland's, and the rocky reefs and kelp forests here are some of the best diving in the state.

Busan, South Korea

Busan is on Korea's southeast coast. The water here is cooler and greener than Catalina's, and most diving runs off small boats from the harbor.

Cebu, Philippines

Fifteen dives over five days, mostly around Mactan Island, plus a trip down the southwest coast to Alegria. Water temperature on the deepest dive was 29–30 °C.
